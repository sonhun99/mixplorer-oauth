Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

1. Overview

MiXplorer Personal is a personal Google OAuth configuration used by the account owner to access Google Drive through the MiXplorer file manager.

2. Data Access

The configuration requests Google Drive access to enable the account owner to view, upload, download, modify, and delete files.

3. Data Collection

The owner of this OAuth configuration does not operate a separate server that collects Google Drive files or account information.

4. Data Storage

Authentication credentials and Google Drive data may be processed or stored by the MiXplorer application according to its implementation and privacy practices.

5. Data Sharing

The owner of this OAuth configuration does not independently sell or share Google Drive data with third parties.

6. Data Security

Access to Google Drive is authorized through Google's OAuth 2.0 authentication system.

7. Access Revocation

The account owner can revoke access through Google Account security settings.

8. Contact

For inquiries, contact the owner of this OAuth configuration.
